Use and Occupancy Classifications. A summary of building occupancy groups under the International Building Code, Chapter 3. See the code book for details.

## Assembly (IBC Section 303)

- Group A-1. Production and viewing of performing arts. Examples: motion picture theaters, symphony and concert halls, TV and radio studios with an audience, theaters.
- Group A-2. Food and/or drink consumption. Examples: banquet halls, night clubs, restaurants, taverns and bars.
- Group A-3. Worship, recreation or amusement and other uses not classified elsewhere in Group A. Examples: amusement arcades, art galleries, bowling, community halls, courtrooms, dance halls, exhibition halls, funeral parlors, gymnasiums (without spectator seating), indoor swimming pools and tennis (without spectator seating), lecture halls, libraries, museums, religious worship, pool halls, waiting areas in transportation terminals.
- Group A-4. Viewing of indoor sports and activities. Examples: arenas, skating rinks, swimming pools, tennis courts (with spectator seating).
- Group A-5. Participation in or viewing of outdoor activities. Examples: amusement park structures, bleachers, grandstands, stadiums.

## Business (IBC Section 304)

- Group B. Office, professional, services. Examples: air traffic control towers, ambulatory health care, animal hospitals and kennels, banks, barber and beauty shops, car washes, civic administration, clinics, dry cleaning and laundry, educational occupancies above 12th grade, data processing, laboratories, motor vehicle showrooms, post offices, print shops, professional services (architects, attorneys, physicians, engineers, etc.), radio and TV, telephone exchanges, training or skill development.

## Educational (IBC Section 305)

- Group E. Through 12th grade. See the code for details.

## Factory and Industrial (IBC Section 306)

Assembling, disassembling, fabricating, finishing, manufacturing, packaging, repair, processing.

- Group F-1. Moderate hazard. See the list in the code book.
- Group F-2. Low hazard. See the list in the code book.

## High Hazard (IBC Section 307)

Manufacturing, processing, generation, or storage of materials that constitute a physical or health hazard.

- Group H-1. Detonation hazards.
- Group H-2. Deflagration or accelerated burning hazards.
- Group H-3. Materials that readily support combustion, or physical hazards.
- Group H-4. Health hazards.
- Group H-5. Hazardous production materials (HPM).

## Institutional (IBC Section 308)

Supervised care or living, medical or other treatment, correctional facilities, etc.

- Group I-1. Examples: alcohol and drug centers, assisted living, congregate care, convalescent facilities, group homes, halfway houses, residential board and care, social rehabilitation.
- Group I-2. Examples: child care, detox, hospitals, mental hospitals, nursing homes.
- Group I-3. Examples: correctional facilities, detention, jails, prerelease centers, prisons, reformatories.
- Group I-4. Day care (all ages).

## Mercantile (IBC Section 309)

- Group M. Display and sale of merchandise. Examples: department stores, drug stores, markets, motor fuel dispensing, retail or wholesale stores, sales rooms.

## Residential (IBC Section 310)

- Group R-1. Transient sleeping units. Examples: boarding houses, hotels, motels.
- Group R-2. Sleeping units or more than 2 dwelling units (permanent). Examples: apartment houses, boarding homes, convents, dormitories, fraternities and sororities, hotels or motels (non-transient), live/work units, monasteries, vacation timeshares.
- Group R-3. Other permanent residential not classified as R-1, R-2 or R-4. See the code book for details.
- Group R-4. Residential care and assisted living, six to sixteen occupants, excluding staff.

## Storage (IBC Section 311)

Storage not classified as a hazardous occupancy.

- Group S-1. Moderate hazard storage. See the code book for details.
- Group S-2. Low hazard storage. See the code book for details.

## Utility and Miscellaneous (IBC Section 312)

- Group U. Miscellaneous and accessory structures not classified in any specific occupancy. Examples: agricultural buildings, aircraft hangars, barns, carports, fences, grain silos (residential), greenhouses, livestock shelters, private garages, retaining walls, sheds, stables, tanks, towers.
