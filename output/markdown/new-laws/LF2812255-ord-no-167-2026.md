# Ord. No. 167-2026: Zoning Map Amendment (NCM)

<!-- page 1 -->

CITY OF WATERVILLE 

CITY of Waterville M A I E

CITY COUNCIL 

ORDINANCE 167- 2026 Effective Date: / 0/ 

Amend the Zoning Map (4 Veteran Court) 

BE IT ENACTED by the City Council of the City of Waterville, as follows: 

THAT, Appendix A (Zoning) Article 2, Section 2. 1. 1., the official zoning map dated April 6, 1999, and the written description of zoning boundaries dated July 6, 1971, be and hereby are amended as follows: 

4 Veteran Court (Assessor Parcel 39- 253) is rezoned from Commercial -B ( C -B) and Residential - C ( R -C) to Residential -C in its entirety. 

# SUMMARY: 

Rezoning will reduce the building setback requirements and allow Richard Wentworth to replace his existing garage with a larger one closer to his property line. 

IN THE CITY COUNCIL, 

September 1 , 2026 First Reading and Accepted. 62-t- 1- S , 2026 Second Reading and Adopted.

MEMO

CITY of Waterville IA A 1 N E

DATE: August 27, 2026 TO: Mayor & City Councilors 

FROM: Ann Beverage, City Planner 

SUBJECT: Planning Board Recommendation on Rezoning 4 Veteran Court 

On August 25, 2025, the Planning Board held a public hearing on a request by Richard Wentworth to rezone his property at 4 Veteran Court from Commercial -B ( C -B) and Residential - C ( R -C) to Residential -C in its entirety. Rezoning will reduce the building setback requirements and allow him to replace his existing garage with a larger one closer to his property line. 

# PUBLIC COMMENT: 

Only one abutting property owner attended the public hearing. Lourdes Carrion, owner of 61 Water Street, supported rezoning because a new garage will improve the property which is directly behind her building. 

# ACTION: 

The Board voted 6- 0 in favor of recommending that the City Council rezone the property as requested.
