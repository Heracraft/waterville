# BOARDS, COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS 

# **City of Waterville Appendix 2B** 

# **Committee Charge: Community Service Funding Advisory Committee [Adopted 12-28-2023 by Ord. No. 199-2023]** 

# **<u>Purpose.</u>** 

The Committee is charged with advising the City Council on allowable uses of federal, state and local funds available by way of grants, gifts, allocations and the operating budget and how it proposes that the funds be allocated among the various eligible categories of recipients. 

In accomplishing its mission, the Committee is being charged to carry out the following tasks: 

- Become familiar with restrictions on use of funds through reading, tutoring by government experts, and input from legal counsel as required 

- Consider establishing an allocation matrix based on eligible uses and eligible recipients or some other appropriate manner of expressing allocation recommendations that addresses the eligible uses in relation to impacted populations in Waterville 

- Inventory the community of public service organizations serving Waterville, their missions, services, demographics of their clientele, size, financial stability, and performance 

- Identify organizations through which funding allocations might be transmitted that will have a multiplying effect on the impact of the funds so allocated 

- Formulate a series of recommendations to the Council for allocations that cite amounts, recipients, purposes, estimated multiplying impact and timelines for distribution and implementation 

- Develop a process for Council consideration that evaluates the return on City investments in services aligned with Council goals. 

- Design a process for consideration by the Council for selecting organizations to fund 

# **<u>Membership.</u>** 

Membership shall be comprised 2 Councilors and an unspecified number of persons, who may or may not be residents of the City, who hold influential positions in the following sectors of the community: 

- Economics, banking, business finance, financial analysis 

- Nonprofit organizations that service Waterville residents, families, and businesses 

- The Waterville faith-based community 

- The Waterville business community 

- Philanthropic organizations that participate in funding Waterville-based projects and programs 

- The Waterville public and private education communities 

- The Waterville health-care community

# **<u>Scope of Authority.</u>** 

The Committee’s role is to advise the Council, and to provide the Council with recommended allocations of funds based on its research, understanding, conclusions with respect to the projected benefits and detriments of proposals, and its professional and personal judgments regarding proposed allocations and uses of the funds. 

The Committee is authorized and expected to reach out to all resources it determines to be valuable to its understanding of the parameters of the use of the funds and to all parties that would be eligible recipients of funding. 

It is not authorized to make promises or enter into verbal or written agreements purporting to represent the city. 

# **<u>Conduct of Business.</u>** 

The Committee is an official body of the City. As such it is subject to Maine’s Freedom of Access Act, 1 M.R.S. § 403, and must meet in a location accessible to the public, allow the public to attend its meetings, publicly post the dates and times of its meetings, and keep minutes for the public record. 

No member of the Committee may represent themselves or a family member, their business interests or those of a business partner, or participate in Committee deliberations or vote on any matter having to do with such interests. When such a conflict occurs, the affected committee member(s) must declare the conflict, recuse themselves from the meeting and vacate the meeting space until the conflict ceases to exist. 

# **<u>Reporting.</u>** 

A Councilor who is a member of the Committee will be called upon at each regular meeting of the Council, or at a frequency otherwise determined by vote of the Council, to report on the Committee’s progress toward offering recommendations to the Council.
