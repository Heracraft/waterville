# Chapter 9. Boards, Committees and Commissions, Attachment 7 - Appendix 1G, Finance Committee

<!-- page 1 -->

BOARDS, COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS 

# **City of Waterville Appendix 1G Committee Charge: Finance Committee [Adopted 12-28-2023 by Ord. No. 199-2023]** 

# **<u>Establishment and Regulation.</u>** 

City of Waterville City Charter, Article VII, § 9 1 M.R.S., Chapter 13, Freedom of Access City of Waterville Ethics Ordinance (Chapter 18) 

# **<u>Purpose.</u>** 

The Committee is charged with promoting communication between the municipal and public school departments. This Committee may advise in the City budget process by allowing its members to exchange information about current and foreseeable fiscal issues. 

# **<u>Membership.</u>** 

Membership shall be comprised of seven members, to include the Superintendent, two School Board members, 2 City Councilors, the City Manager, and the Mayor. 

# **<u>Scope of Authority.</u>** 

The Committee is advisory in nature and its role is to promote communication between the City and School Department’s management team and its elected officials in order to make decisions regarding budgeting, capital projects, and borrowing. 

The Committee is not authorized to make promises or enter into verbal or written agreements purporting to represent the City. 

# **<u>Conduct of Business.</u>** 

The Committee is an official body of the City. As such it is subject to Maine’s Freedom of Access Act, 1 M.R.S. § 403, and must meet in a location accessible to the public, allow the public to attend its meetings, publicly post the dates and times of its meetings, and keep minutes for the public record. 

No member of the Committee may participate in a matter before the Committee where that member has a conflict of interest. If a question arises as to whether a member has a conflict of interest, after disclosure, the remaining members shall make that determination. The Committee may review the Ethics Ordinance or consult with the Solicitor on conflict of interest questions. 

# **<u>Reporting.</u>** 

The Committee is required to present quarterly reports to the City Council and Board of Education that detail the financial condition of the municipal and school departments.
