# Chapter 227. Smoking

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the City Council of the City of Waterville 5-1-2012 by Ord. No. 98-2012. Amendments noted where applicable.]*

### § 227-1. Definitions.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**ELECTRONIC SMOKING DEVICE**: A device used to deliver nicotine or any other substance intended for human consumption that may be used by a person to simulate smoking through inhalation of vapor or aerosol from the device, including, without limitation, a device manufactured, distributed, marketed or sold as an electronic cigarette, electronic cigar, electronic pipe, electronic hookah or so-called "vape pen."

**SMOKING**: Includes carrying or having in one's possession a lighted or heated cigarette, cigar, or pipe or a lighted or heated tobacco or plant product intended for human consumption through inhalation, whether natural or synthetic in any manner or in any form. "Smoking" includes the use of an electronic smoking device.



### § 227-2. No-smoking areas.

Smoking is prohibited in the following City recreation areas and adjacent public parking lots and sidewalks:

- A. North Street from the Alfond Center to the Municipal Boat Landing, inclusive.

- B. Recreation areas on Chaplin Street, Green Street/Sherwin Street, Grove Street, Hillside Avenue, Kelsey Street, Moor Street, Sterling Street and Western Avenue.

### § 227-3. Enforcement; violations and penalties.

Prior to issuing a citation for violation of this chapter, a law enforcement officer shall issue one verbal warning to an individual. If the individual fails to comply after the warning, the individual shall be issued a citation to appear in court. If the individual is found to be in violation of this chapter, the court shall impose a fine of $50, which may not be suspended.
