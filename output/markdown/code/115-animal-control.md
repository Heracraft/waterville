# Chapter 115. Animal Control

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the City Council of the City of Waterville 9-30-2006 by Ord. No. 14-2006; last amended 12-21-2021 by Ord. No. 259-2021.[^1] Subsequent amendments noted where applicable.]*

[^1]: Editor's Note: This chapter was previously amended 7-28-2021 by Ord. No. 134-2021.

## Article I. Animals in General

### § 115-1. Feeding of birds.

No person shall feed or bait any bird upon any public property within the congested business area, as defined in Chapter 173, Licenses and Permits, of the City Code.

## Article II. Dogs

### § 115-2. Definitions.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

For the purpose of this article, the following terms shall have the meanings given herein:

**AT LARGE**: Off the premises of the owner and not under the control of any person by means of a leash or personal presence and attention as will reasonably control the conduct of such dog.

**DOG**: Shall be intended to mean both male and female.

**OWNER**: Shall be intended to mean any person or persons, firm, association or corporation owning, keeping or harboring a dog; the parent or guardian of a minor who owns, keeps or has in his/her possession a dog.

### § 115-3. License required.

Each owner of a dog of the age of six months or older must secure a license for such from the City Clerk pursuant to all rules and regulations set forth in 7 M.R.S. § 3921 et seq.

### § 115-4. Running at large.

Any dog running at large may be seized and impounded. The owner of such dog commits a civil violation for which a forfeiture of not less than $25 nor more than $100 shall be adjudged, no portion of which shall be suspended or deferred.

### § 115-5. Number of dogs limited.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

- A. Except in the Rural Residential (R-R) Zone, it shall be unlawful for any person to keep or harbor within the City more than four dogs over four months old in or about any premises, house, barn or other building, or in or about all buildings on any one premises occupied by any one family, and the keeping or harboring of dogs as aforesaid is hereby declared to be a nuisance. In Rural Residential (R-R) Zones, the limit shall be 10 dogs. (More than 10 dogs shall be deemed "animal husbandry," which is regulated by Chapter 275, Zoning, § 275-4.5.

- B. The payment for a license or licenses on dogs shall not be construed to allow the keeping of more than the number of allowed dogs, as aforesaid, on any one premises.

- C. This limitation shall not apply to any person engaged in the commercial business of breeding, buying, selling or boarding of dogs, or operating a veterinary hospital as long as any such operation complies with all zoning and other City ordinances and state licensing requirements.

### § 115-6. Nuisance dogs.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

A dog shall be considered a nuisance if it:

- A. Damages, soils, defiles or defecates on public walks and recreation areas, or on private property other than property owned by the dog owner, unless such waste is immediately removed and properly disposed of by the dog owner and any damage is corrected by the dog owner;

- B. Causes unsanitary, dangerous or offensive conditions;

- C. Causes a disturbance by excessive barking or other noise-making, unless it is an agricultural working dog exempt in accordance with 7 M.R.S. § 3950;

- D. Chases vehicles;

- E. Molests, attacks or otherwise interferes with any other person or domestic animal on property not owned by the dog owner; or

- F. Is not leashed when not on private property.

### § 115-7. Remedy for nuisance dogs.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

- A. This chapter shall be enforced by the Animal Control Officer of the Waterville Police.

- B. The owner of any dog found to be a nuisance shall be in violation of this chapter. If the Animal Control Officer of the Waterville Police determines that a dog is a nuisance, the owner shall be immediately notified and given the opportunity to correct the nuisance.

- C. Failure to correct the nuisance or otherwise comply with this chapter is a civil violation for which is imposed a forfeiture of not less than $50 for a first offense and $100 for a second or subsequent offense within the same calendar year.
