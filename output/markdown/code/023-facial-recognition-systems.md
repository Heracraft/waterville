# Chapter 23. Facial Recognition Systems

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the City Council of the City of Waterville 6-1-2021 by Ord. No. 118-2021. Amendments noted where applicable.]*

### § 23-1. Definitions.

For the purposes of this chapter, the following terms have the following meanings:

**CITY OF WATERVILLE**: Any department, agency, bureau and/or subordinate division of the City of Waterville.

**CITY OF WATERVILLE OFFICIAL**: Any person or entity acting on behalf of the City of Waterville, including any officer, employee, agent, contractor, subcontractor or vendor.

**FACIAL RECOGNITION**: An automated or semi-automated process that assists in identifying or verifying individuals, or captures information about them, based on the physical characteristics of their faces.

**FACIAL RECOGNITION SYSTEM**: Any computer software or application that performs facial recognition.

### § 23-2. Facial recognition technology restricted.

- A. The City of Waterville and City officials are prohibited from:

  - (1) Obtaining or possessing any facial recognition system.

  - (2) Entering into a contract or other agreement with any third party for the purpose of obtaining or possessing any facial recognition system.

- B. The City of Waterville and City officials may utilize the facial recognition systems of other entities, public or private, for the purpose of performing law enforcement functions.

### § 23-3. Enforcement.

Any City employee who violates a provision of this chapter may be subject to discipline in accordance with the City's Personnel Ordinance[^1] and applicable collective bargaining agreements.

[^1]: Editor's Note: See Ch. 56, Personnel.

### § 23-4. Exceptions and safe harbors.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to:

- A. Limit any individual's rights under state or federal law;

- B. Prohibit the use of an automated or semi-automated process for the purpose of redacting a recording for release or disclosure to protect the privacy of a subject depicted in the recording;

- C. Prohibit the use of facial recognition or similar biometric technique on privately owned consumer devices for personal use or security, or for commercial use or security; or

- D. Prohibit the use of facial recognition or similar biometric technique in managing secure entry or access to restricted buildings, rooms or other secure spaces, devices or things.
