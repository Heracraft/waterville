# Chapter 127. Building and Energy Code

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the City Council of the City of Waterville effective 9-30-2006 by Ord. No. 17-2006 and Ord. No. 18-2006; last amended 8-27-2024 by Ord. No. 145-2024.[^2] Subsequent amendments noted where applicable.]*

[^1]: The title of this chapter, formerly "Building and Electrical Code," was amended 11-18-2025 by Ord. No. 209-2025.

[^2]: Editor's Note: This chapter was previously amended 5-15-2018 by Ord. No. 86-2018 and 6-1-2021 by Ord. No. 133-2021.

## Article I. In General

### § 127-1. Building Code adopted; scope.

Pursuant to the authority granted in 30-A M.R.S. § 3001, the City hereby adopts the Maine Uniform Building and Energy Code (MUBEC) for the purpose of establishing rules and regulations for the construction, alteration, removal or demolition of buildings and structures, including permit fees and penalties, and all the provisions of said code are incorporated as fully as if set out at length herein. This code, as amended from time to time, will be kept on file in the office of the Code Enforcement Officer, and it is understood that the provisions of said code shall be controlling in the construction of all buildings and structures within the corporate limits of the City.

### § 127-2. Building permits required.

- A. Permits are required prior to the start of any construction, alteration, removal or demolition of any and all buildings or structures within the City of Waterville and as further explained in the duly adopted Maine Uniform Building and Energy Code (MUBEC), as amended from time to time.

- B. All permits required by this article shall be issued by the City's Office of Code Enforcement.

### § 127-3. Fees.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

- A. Fees are as provided for in the Maine Uniform Building and Energy Code (MUBEC) and as set forth in the fee schedule available from the City offices and/or on the City website. The City Council reserves the right to amend said fee structure from time to time.

- B. Permits applied for after work has started may be charged double the amount of the fee as prescribed above, subject to a maximum of $500. Failure to acquire a permit within 30 calendar days of the issuance of a violation notice from the Code Enforcement Officer shall cause such fee to be increased to $1,000. Such assessed penalty shall not absolve the violator of any responsibilities or fines as a result of other violations associated with this project.

### § 127-4. Municipal review of development.

(Ref. Chapter 210, Public Safety, Article I, Fire Prevention, § 210-4.)

- A. In accordance with Title 25 M.R.S., Part 6, Chapter 317, § 2448-A, the Fire Chief is authorized to conduct municipal review of all development and assess fees consistent with Maine law.

- B. Any fees collected by the City of Waterville on behalf of the State of Maine as outlined in 25 M.R.S. § 2450 will subsequently be paid to the state.

- C. Prior to the construction of a new building, alteration, modification, or change of occupancy, the Fire Chief or Chief's designee shall have the authority to require an inspection of the activity site prior to approval of a construction permit application.

- D. The Fire Chief is authorized to conduct a review of all sprinkler and fire alarm installations, alterations, and modifications, prior to installation.

### § 127-5. Duties of Code Enforcement Officer; records.

- A. The Code Enforcement Officer (CEO) shall receive the applications required by this code and issue permits and any other certifications required herein. The CEO shall examine premises for which permits have been issued, and shall make necessary inspections to see that the provisions of law are complied with and that construction proceeds safely. The CEO shall, when requested by proper authority, or when the public interest so requires, make investigations in connection with matters referred to in the Code and render written reports on them. To enforce compliance with law, to remove illegal or unsafe conditions, to secure the necessary safeguards during construction or to require adequate exit facilities in building and structures, the CEO shall issue such notices or orders as may be necessary.

- B. Inspections required under the provisions of the Building Code shall be made by the CEO or his duly appointed assistant. The CEO may require reports from inspectors of recognized inspection services, after investigation of their qualifications and reliability. No certificate called for by any provision of the Building Code shall be issued on such reports unless they are in writing and certified to by a responsible officer of such service.

- C. The CEO shall keep comprehensive records of applications, of permits or certificates issued, of inspections made, of reports rendered and of required plans and all documents relating to building work so long as any part of the building or structure to which they relate may be in existence.

- D. All such records shall be open to public inspection for good and sufficient reasons at the stated office hours, but shall not be removed from the office of the CEO without the CEO's consent.

### § 127-6. Certificates of occupancy.

If requested, the Code Enforcement Officer shall issue a certificate of occupancy after the building meets all requirements of the Maine Uniform Building and Energy Code (MUBEC) and the National Life Safety Code.

### § 127-7. Violations and penalties.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

Penalties for violations of the Maine Uniform Building and Energy Code (MUBEC) shall be in accordance with the civil penalties provided in 30-A M.R.S. § 4452(3), as amended from time to time.

## Article II. Electrical Requirements

### § 127-8. Code adopted; scope.

There is hereby adopted by the City under authority of 30-A M.R.S. § 3003 for the purpose of establishing rules and regulations for all electrical installations a certain electrical code known as the "National Electrical Code" (current edition adopted by the State of Maine), and sometimes referred to as "NFPA Standard 70," as amended from time to time, a copy of which is filed in the office of the Code Enforcement Officer; and the same is hereby adopted and incorporated as fully as if set out at length herein, and the provisions thereof shall be controlling in the installation of all electrical wiring and equipment within the corporate limits of the City.

### § 127-9. Electrical Inspector.

The City Manager shall appoint a Municipal Electrical Inspector, who shall be a person possessing a practical knowledge of electricity. The Municipal Electrical Inspector shall be the person charged with the enforcement of this article. Electrical permitting and inspection requirements of this article shall be applicable to all the following, within the territorial boundaries of the City of Waterville:

- A. All original installation of electrical equipment at single-family and two-family occupancies;

- B. Alterations or additions to existing electrical equipment at single-family and two-family occupancies.

### § 127-10. Municipal electrical permits required; fees.

*[Amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

- A. As provided for in § 127-2, municipal electrical permits are required prior to the start of any construction work on single-family and two-family occupancies. Fees for such permits are set forth in the fee schedule available from the City offices and/or on the City website, which fee structure may be amended from time to time by the City Council. Permitting and inspections for all other electrical work within the City of Waterville shall be performed in accordance with 32 M.R.S., Chapter 17, Subchapter 1, § 1102-C.

- B. Permits applied for after work has started may be charged double the amount of the fee as prescribed above, subject to a maximum of $500. Failure to acquire a permit within 30 calendar days of the issuance of a violation notice from the Code Enforcement Officers shall cause such fee to be increased to $1,000. Such assessed penalty shall not absolve the violator of any responsibilities or fines as a result of other violations associated with this project.
