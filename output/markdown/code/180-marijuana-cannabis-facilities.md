# Chapter 180. Marijuana/Cannabis Facilities

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the City Council of the City of Waterville as indicated in article histories. Amendments noted where applicable.[^1]]*

[^1]: Editor's Note: This chapter was amended 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025 to change "marijuana" to "cannabis" throughout.

## Article I. Dispensaries and Primary Caregiver Operations

*[Adopted 7-19-2016 by Ord. No. 132-2016]*

### § 180-1. Location restrictions.

There shall be no cannabis dispensaries or primary caregiver operations in the following downtown area: bordered on the north by Union Street, on the south by Spring Street, on the east by the Kennebec River and on the west by Elm Street, inclusive; also, the properties with frontage on Water Street between Spring Street and Sherwin Street.

### § 180-1.1. Definitions.

*[Added 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

As used in this article, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:

**CAREGIVER**: A person or an assistant of that person that provides care for a qualifying patient in accordance with 22 M.R.S. § 2423-A, Subsection 2.

**DISPENSARY**: An entity registered under 22 M.R.S. § 2425-A that acquires, possesses, cultivates, manufactures, delivers, transfers, transports, sells, supplies or dispenses cannabis plants or harvested cannabis or related supplies and educational materials to qualifying patients and the caregivers of those patients.

## Article II. Cannabis Ordinance

*[Adopted 4-2-2019 by Ord. No. 56-2019; last amended 12-21-2021 by Ord. No. 257-2021[^1]]*

[^1]: Editor's Note: This article was also amended 10-15-2019 by Ord. No. 172-2019; 2-4-2020 by Ord. No. 25-2020; 10-20-2020 by Ord. No. 221-2020; and 10-5-2021 by Ord. No. 207-2021.

### § 180-2. Title and scope.

This article shall be known and cited as the "City of Waterville Cannabis Ordinance" and will be referred to hereinafter as "this article." This article limits all adult use and medical cannabis establishments to the zoning rules and regulations contained herein and to the districts specified.

### § 180-3. Findings and intent.

- A. Implementing a system for the regulation of establishments for the sale, cultivation, manufacturing and testing for both adult use and medical cannabis is a complex function with significant administrative demands on the City of Waterville.

- B. The purpose of this article is to ensure that possession and use of adult use and medical cannabis are limited to persons who are 21 years of age or older, except in the case of minors in possession of a medical cannabis patient card.

- C. The City of Waterville believes that any production, testing, processing or selling of adult use and medical cannabis should be conducted in a safe and fair manner for the health, safety and welfare of the community, which includes complying with provisions of all applicable laws and ordinances relating to adult use and medical cannabis throughout the City of Waterville.

- D. Therefore, this article is adopted pursuant to the Cannabis Legalization Act, 28-B M.R.S. Ch. 1; Maine's medical cannabis laws and regulations; Article VIII, Part 2, Section 1 of the Maine Constitution; the provisions of the Municipal Home Rule Authority (30-A M.R.S. § 3001 et seq.); and the provisions of the Planning and Land Use Regulation Act, 30-A M.R.S. § 4312 et seq.

### § 180-4. Definitions.

The terms in this article shall have the same definitions as those contained in state statutes regulating medical and adult use cannabis.

### § 180-5. License required; qualifications; exemptions.

- A. No person may establish, operate or maintain an adult use, medical cannabis establishment or registered dispensary without first obtaining a license from the City Clerk.

- B. The holder of a license for a medical cannabis store located in the following zoning districts, Commercial-B, Commercial-C, Commercial-D or General Industrial, with the exception of the area east of Front Street, may exchange the license for an adult use cannabis store license in the same location. Said holder must file an application with a nonrefundable application fee of $500 and pay the appropriate license fee.

- C. The holder of a license for an adult use cannabis store located in the following districts, Commercial-B, Commercial-C, Commercial-D or General Industrial, may exchange their license for a medical cannabis store license in the same location. Said holder must file an application with the application fee of $500 and pay the appropriate license fee. Applicants who are not approved to receive a license will be refunded the license fee, but not the application fee.

- D. All adult use, medical cannabis stores and registered dispensaries must be operated from permanent locations, which may utilize telephone and internet orders.

- E. Adult use, medical cannabis stores and registered dispensaries may not use vending machines for sales.

- F. All licenses will expire one year from the date of issuance.

- G. Exempt from licensing are:

  - (1) A person who cultivates cannabis exclusively for that person's own use or an immediate family member's use who is living within their household.

  - (2) A caregiver that is registered as "noncultivating" under state law.

  - (3) Any outdoor cultivation operations.

  - (4) Any cultivation taking place in a residential zone.

### § 180-6. Application procedure and fees.

- A. All applications for licenses must be made on the form provided by the City Clerk in order to determine whether the applicant meets the requirements of this article and requires a one-time application fee of $500. Prior to application to the City Clerk, the applicant must first obtain conditional approval from the appropriate state agency.

- B. Application and annual license fees. All applications must be submitted with the appropriate application fees and, if approved, the following annual license fees must be paid before the City will issue a license:

  - (1) Cannabis stores, license fee: $5,000.

  - (2) Registered dispensaries, license fee: $5,000.

  - (3) Cannabis manufacturing/extraction facility, license fee: $1,500.

  - (4) Cannabis manufacturing/consumable facility, license fee: $100.

  - (5) Cannabis testing facility, license fee: $1,500.

  - (6) Cannabis cultivation:

    - (a) Tiers.

      - [1] Tier 1: up to 30 mature plants (and an unlimited number of immature plants and seedlings) or up to 500 square feet of plant canopy: $500.

      - [2] Tier 2: 501 square feet to 2,000 square feet of mature plant canopy, license fee: $2,500.

      - [3] Tier 3: 2,001 square feet to 7,000 square feet of mature plant canopy, license fee: $4,000.

      - [4] Tier 4: 7,001 square feet to 20,000 square feet of mature plant canopy, license fee: $7,500.

    - (b) Every two years, Tier 4 renewal applicants that are already at 20,000 square feet may seek a 7,000 square feet plant canopy increase if in compliance with applicable state law. Each two-year increase will require a license fee increase of $2,000.

    - (c) Renewal applicants for cannabis cultivation licenses may seek an increase to a higher tier if they comply with the requirements in this section.

    - (d) Applicants for cannabis cultivation licenses may not hold more than three such licenses or a total combined plant canopy in excess of 30,000 square feet.

  - (7) Caregivers who are licensed under Maine law for indoor cultivation which are operating exclusively in commercial or industrial zones: $500.

### § 180-7. Location restrictions.

*[Amended 5-2-2023 by Ord. No. 75-2023; 5-20-2025 by Ord. No. 101-2025]*

- A. Adult use, medical cannabis retail stores, registered dispensaries, and generally safe extraction facilities as defined by the Office of Cannabis Policy may only exist and operate in the Commercial-B (C-B), Commercial-C (C-C), Commercial-D (C-D), General Industrial (IND) and Airport Industrial (A-I) Zoning Districts.

- B. Adult use and medical cannabis potentially hazardous and inherently hazardous extraction facilities as defined by the Office of Cannabis Policy may only exist and operate in the General Industrial (IND) and Airport Industrial (A-I) Zoning Districts.

- C. Adult use and medical cannabis testing facilities may only exist and operate in the Commercial-B (C-B), Commercial-C (C-C), Commercial-D (C-D), General Industrial (IND) and Airport Industrial (A-I) Zoning Districts.

- D. Adult use and medical cannabis cultivation facilities may only exist and operate in the Rural Residential (R-R), Commercial-C (C-C), General Industrial (IND) and Airport Industrial (A-I) Zoning Districts.

- E. Adult use, medical cannabis establishments, and registered dispensaries may not be operated on property located within:

  - (1) Five hundred feet of the property line of a public or preexisting private school (K-12) per 28-B M.R.S. § 402(2)(A); and/or

  - (2) Five hundred feet of the property line of a property used primarily for religious worship and related religious activities; property used for preexisting licensed day-care use under 10-148 CMR c. 32; or recreational areas designated for use by children up to 18 years in age; and 400 feet of the property line of areas designated as municipal "safe zones" pursuant to 30-A M.R.S. § 3253 and Chapter 210, Article IV, Safe Zones, of the City Code.

- F. Required setbacks under Subsection E(1) and (2) above shall be measured as the most direct, level, shortest straight-line distance between property lines of a property containing a building or structure used as the premises where an adult use or medical cannabis establishment is conducted to the nearest property line of the premises of a use listed in Subsection E shown above.

### § 180-8. Preexisting facilities.

Medical cannabis establishments located and operating within Waterville under and in compliance with Maine's medical cannabis laws and regulations on or before December 13, 2018, which file applications for an adult use or medical cannabis establishment license for locations that they currently operate within and have continuously operated within since December 13, 2018, are exempt from § 180-7E of this article.

### § 180-9. Security measures.

Security measures at all adult use, medical cannabis establishment and registered dispensary premises shall include, at a minimum, the following:

- A. Security surveillance cameras installed and operating 24 hours a day, seven days a week, with ten-day video storage, to monitor all entrances, along with the interior and exterior of the premises, to discourage and facilitate the reporting of criminal acts and nuisance activities occurring at the premises; and

- B. Door and window combination video and motion-detector intrusion system with audible alarm, maintained in good working condition; and

- C. A locking safe affixed to the premises that is suitable for storage of all cannabis, cannabis products and cash stored overnight on the licensed premises; and

- D. Exterior lighting that illuminates the exterior walls of the licensed premises during dusk to dawn, that is either constantly on or activated by motion detectors, and complies with applicable provisions of the lighting performance standards in Chapter 275, Zoning, of the City of Waterville Code; and

- E. Deadbolt locks on all exterior doors and any other exterior access points; except windows, which shall have locks; and

- F. For adult use facilities, methods to ensure that no person under the age of 21 shall have access to cannabis and cannabis products.

### § 180-10. Ventilation.

All adult use, medical cannabis and registered dispensary businesses are required to be in compliance with any odor nuisance control and abatement performance standard in Chapter 275, Zoning, of the City Code.

### § 180-11. Required notices.

There shall be posted in a conspicuous location inside each adult use, medical cannabis and registered dispensary establishment at least one legible sign containing the following information:

- A. "Adult use of or on-site consumption of cannabis is illegal.

  Open and public consumption of cannabis in the State of Maine is illegal.

  The use of cannabis or cannabis products may impair a person's ability to drive a car or operate machinery.

  No one under the age of 21 allowed.

  Loitering prohibited."

- B. Medical use:

  "Use of or on site consumption of cannabis is illegal.

  Open and public consumption of cannabis in the State of Maine is illegal.

  The use of cannabis or cannabis products may impair a person's ability to drive a car or operate machinery.

  No one under the age of 21 allowed, except a minor with a medical cannabis card between the ages of 18 and 21, or a minor under 18 years of age accompanied by a legal guardian;

  Loitering prohibited."

### § 180-12. Signs.

- A. All signs used by, and all marketing and advertising conducted by or on behalf of the cannabis establishment and registered dispensary may not involve advertising or marketing that has a high likelihood of reaching persons under 21 years of age or that is specifically designed to appeal particularly to persons under 21 years of age. The signs, marketing or advertising is prohibited from making any health or physical benefit claims.

- B. All signage shall meet the requirements for signs under Chapter 275, Zoning, of the City Code.

### § 180-13. Right of access; background checks; inspections.

- A. Every adult use, medical cannabis establishment and registered dispensary shall allow Code Enforcement Officers to enter the premises at reasonable times for the purpose of checking compliance with all applicable state laws and this article.

- B. Due to fire, explosion and other hazards inherent in cannabis cultivation and manufacturing facilities and registered dispensaries, including, but not limited to, heavy electrical loads, hot lighting fixtures, CO^2 enrichment, extraction solvents (acetone, butane, propane, ethanol, heptane, isopropanol, CO^2, etc.), high-pressure extraction methods (CO^2, etc.) and flammable contents, the owners of all such facilities shall agree to be inspected annually by the Waterville Fire Department.

- C. Cannabis cultivation and manufacturing facilities and registered dispensaries must comply with NFPA-1 Chapter 38 standards for cannabis growing, processing or extraction facilities, and these standards will be utilized by the Waterville Fire Department during its inspections of these premises.

### § 180-14. Indemnification.

- A. By accepting a license issued pursuant to this article, the licensee waives and releases the City, its officers, elected officials, employees, attorneys and agents from any liability for injuries, damages or liabilities of any kind that result from any arrest or prosecution of any adult use, medical cannabis establishment or registered dispensary owners, operators, employees, clients or customers for a violation of local, state or federal laws, rules or regulations.

- B. By accepting a license issued pursuant to this article, the licensee agrees to indemnify, defend and hold harmless the City, its officers, elected officials, employees, attorneys, agents and insurers against all liability, claims and demands on account of any injury, loss or damage, including, without limitation, claims arising from bodily injury, personal injury, sickness, disease, death, property loss or damage, or any other loss of any kind whatsoever arising out of or in any manner connected with the operation of a licensed adult use or medical cannabis establishment.

### § 180-15. Applicability of state law.

- A. In the event the State of Maine adopts any additional or stricter law or regulation governing the sale, cultivation, manufacture, distribution or testing of adult use or medical cannabis or cannabis products, the additional or stricter regulation shall control the establishment or operation of any adult use or medical cannabis establishment, registered dispensary, cultivation facility, manufacturing facility or testing facility in Waterville.

- B. Compliance with 28-B M.R.S. ch. 1, state laws and regulations pertaining to medical cannabis, and any other applicable state laws or regulations, shall be deemed an additional requirement for issuance or denial of any license under this article, and noncompliance with 28-B M.R.S. ch. 1 and any other applicable state law or regulation shall be grounds for revocation or suspension of any license issued hereunder.

### § 180-16. Enforcement; violations and penalties.

- A. Violations.

  - (1) Any person, including, but not limited to, an adult use or medical cannabis establishment or registered dispensary owner, a property owner where such business is located, or any agent or contractor for same, who orders or conducts any activity in violation of this article shall be subject to a minimum civil penalty of $100 and a maximum civil penalty of $500. Each day a violation continues shall constitute a separate offense.

  - (2) In addition to the civil penalty, the City is entitled to injunctive relief.

  - (3) In any successful prosecution, the City shall be rewarded its reasonable attorney's fees.

  - (4) The City is authorized to enter into administrative consent agreements for the purpose of eliminating violations of this article and recovering fines without court action.

  - (5) Actions for the violation of this article may be initiated by the Code Enforcement Officer or City Solicitor.

- B. Code Enforcement Officer (CEO). The primary enforcement person for the City shall be the Code Enforcement Officer. If the CEO finds that any provision of this article is being violated, the CEO shall notify in writing the person responsible for such violation, indicating the nature of the violation, and ordering the action necessary to correct it, including, but not limited to, discontinuance of illegal use of land, buildings or structures, or work being done, removal of illegal buildings or structures, and abatement or mitigation of violations. A copy of such notices shall be maintained as a permanent record.

- C. Law enforcement officers. Law enforcement officers may at any reasonable time conduct on-site inspections of adult-use establishments to ensure compliance with all applicable laws and conditions attached to license approvals and shall investigate all complaints of alleged violations of this article.

### § 180-17. Conflict with other ordinances; severability.

- A. Whenever a provision of this article conflicts with or is inconsistent with other provisions of this article, or of any other ordinance, regulation or standard, the more restrictive provision shall apply.

- B. Should any section or provision of this article be declared by any court to be invalid, such decision shall not invalidate any other section or provision of this article.

### § 180-18. Confidentiality of applications.

All applications submitted by registered caregivers under this article shall be confidential and only provided to City staff for the performance of their duties.
