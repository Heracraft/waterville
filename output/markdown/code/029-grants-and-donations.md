# Chapter 29. Grants and Donations

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the City Council of the City of Waterville 8-13-2009 by Ord. No. 6-2009, as last amended 7-15-2014 by Ord. No. 111-2014.[^1] Subsequent amendments noted where applicable.]*

[^1]: Editor's Note: This chapter was previously amended 7-29-2010 by Ord. No. 5-2010.

### § 29-1. Purpose.

The purpose of this chapter is to regulate the acceptance of grants or donations of money or property by the City of Waterville.

### § 29-2. Acceptance terms and procedures.

- A. Acceptance of grants or donations of money or property valued at more than $10,000 shall require approval by the City Council by order.

- B. For those grants or donations of $10,000 or less in value, the City's Finance Director is authorized to accept and expend the funds for the purpose stated in the donation, provided that said purpose is consistent with the City's mission of providing municipal services. If the Finance Director declines to accept a grant or donation, the grant or donation shall be placed on the next Council agenda for possible approval.

### § 29-3. Reporting.

The Finance Director/City Manager shall report any new donations or grants to the City Council on a quarterly basis.
