# Chapter 109. Airport

*[HISTORY: Adopted by the City Council of the City of Waterville effective 3-23-2007 by Ord. No. 3-2007; amended in its entirety 8-2-2022 by Ord. No. 166-2022. Subsequent amendments noted where applicable.]*

## Article I. Administration

### § 109-1. Airport Manager.

- A. The City, through its City Manager, will appoint a person to act as the Airport Manager.

- B. Responsibilities of this position will vary according to whether or not the City has a fixed-based operator (FBO) stationed at the airport. At a minimum, the Airport Manager will be responsible for all operational and planning needs of the airport. This could include the supervision of personnel, the purchase of equipment and supplies, preparation of the annual budget and planning for capital improvement projects.

- C. In addition, this person will be the contact person for all state and federal regulatory agencies on matters pertaining to the airport.

### § 109-2. Air-space easements.

Appendix A provides a listing of air space easements required for the operation of the Robert LaFleur municipal airport.[^1]

## Article II. Safety Rules and Regulations

### § 109-3. Purpose.

The purpose of this article is to promote public safety and to establish aircraft taxi and vehicle speed limits on the appropriate portions of the airport.

### § 109-4. Aircraft taxi speeds.

Aircraft taxi speeds shall not exceed 20 miles per hour on the ramps and taxiways of said airport. Taxi tests, where speeds exceeding 20 miles per hour may be necessary, shall be conducted on the runways after receiving prior permission from the Airport Manager or his authorized representative.

### § 109-5. Speed of vehicles.

- A. The speed limits on the airport proper, taxiways, ramps, etc. shall be 15 miles per hour.

- B. There is no attempt to establish speed limits on the runway proper; however, vehicles operating on the runway shall be operated at safe and reasonable speeds.

### § 109-6. Operation of vehicles on ramps, taxiways, runways.

- A. No vehicles, including motorized recreation vehicles and motorized terrain vehicles, shall operate on the ramps, taxiways, runways or any other portions of the airport proper without the permission of the Airport Manager or his authorized representative.

- B. Vehicles must have their headlights on while operating in the areas designated in this section.

### § 109-7. Takeoffs and landings.

The entire airport or designated runways may be closed by the Airport Manager during inclement weather conditions or for other reasons. All regulations published by the Federal Aviation Administration shall apply during such times.

### § 109-8. Calm wind runway.

- A. Runway "5-23" is designated as the calm wind runway. Runway "5-23" shall be used by all arriving and/or departing aircraft whenever the surface wind is less than five nautical miles per hour.

- B. Any pilot who deviates from this procedure shall yield to conflicting traffic which is conforming to this section, and remain clear of the traffic pattern or runway until the way is clear. The intent is to not disrupt aircraft operations conforming to the calm wind runway procedure.

- C. In the event of an emergency, the pilot may elect to use any runway and claim priority by broadcasting the appropriate radio signal or phrase.

### § 109-9. Animals prohibited.

Animals shall not be permitted to roam freely on the airport proper. Any animal which is observed on the runways, taxiways, ramps or grassy areas shall be apprehended. Any costs incurred shall be the responsibility of the owner of the animal or animals.

### § 109-10. Pedestrian traffic.

Pedestrian traffic shall not be permitted on the ramps, or taxiways, or within 500 feet of the runways, without the permission of the Airport Manager or his authorized representative.

### § 109-11. Parking of aircraft.

The Airport Manager or his authorized representative shall be responsible for directing all aircraft to suitable parking spaces.

### § 109-12. Aircraft operational tests and engine run-ups.

Aircraft operational tests or engine run-ups shall be made in the designated areas. In no case will these tests or engine run-ups be performed in the vicinity of the terminal building, any hangar or other inhabited structures.

### § 109-13. Restriction of use of ramps, taxiways or runways.

The Airport Manager, or his authorized representative, shall have the authority to restrict the use of any ramps, taxiways or runways to any person whom he deems to be incapable of safely operating on these areas.

### § 109-14. Inspection of taxiways, ramps and runways.

- A. The Airport Manager, or his authorized representative, shall be responsible for inspecting the taxiways, ramps and runways. This inspection shall be conducted daily.

- B. The Airport Manager is responsible for initiating appropriate NOTAMS in the event any abnormal condition is noted during the inspection. In addition, the Airport Manager is responsible for notifying the City Manager of any abnormal conditions, whether NOTAM action is required or not.

### § 109-15. Reporting of accidents.

The Airport Manager or his authorized representative shall notify the City Manager of any accident which occurs at the airport, or its vicinity, as soon as practicable. A follow-up written report shall be rendered within 72 hours.

### § 109-16. Restrictions for safe conduct of airport activity.

The Airport Manager, or his authorized representative, shall have the authority to suspend operations, close any building, or order from the premises any person or persons whom he deems to be under the influence of intoxicants or drugs.

### § 109-17. Smoking.

Smoking is prohibited within the fence.
